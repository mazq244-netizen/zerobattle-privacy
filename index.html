Zero Battle

Privacy Policy — Last updated: May 2025

Overview

Zero Battle is a reaction-time game. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

Data We Collect

Zero Battle collects minimal data necessary to run the game:

  • Player profile — username and chosen character emoji (set by you)
  • Game stats — reaction times, XP, scores, and streak counts
  • Leaderboard entries — your username and best scores, stored on our server to power the global leaderboard

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • No real name, email address, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No payment information
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs

How Data Is Stored

Your player profile and game stats are stored locally on your device. Leaderboard data (username + scores only) is stored on a secure server solely to display global rankings within the app.

Third-Party Services

Zero Battle does not use any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking services. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

Children

Zero Battle is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
